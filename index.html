VoxaPair Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026 · Developer: VoxaPair

English简体中文

English

This policy explains how VoxaPair handles information when you use the iOS app. VoxaPair provides speech recognition, translation, speech playback, advertising in the free version, and an optional one-time purchase to remove ads.

Speech and translation

With your permission, VoxaPair accesses the microphone to capture speech while you use the recording feature. The app uses Apple's system frameworks for speech recognition, translation, and speech playback. It displays the recognized and translated text in the app. VoxaPair does not operate a server that receives or stores your recordings, transcripts, or translations. Audio and text are not provided by VoxaPair to Google for advertising. The app may ask iOS to download language resources from Apple.

Settings and purchases

The selected language pair and resource-preparation state are stored on your device. VoxaPair does not require an account. The optional permanent ad-removal purchase is handled by Apple's StoreKit. Apple processes payment information; VoxaPair checks the purchase entitlement to decide whether to show ads. VoxaPair does not operate a separate payment server or receive your card details.

Ads and data collected by partners

The free version uses Google AdMob to display a banner ad and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable ad-consent choices. Depending on your location, consent, and device settings, these services may collect or process data such as an IP-derived approximate location, a device or app identifier, ads viewed, app interactions, crash information, performance information, and other diagnostic information. These data may be used for third-party advertising, the developer's advertising or marketing, analytics, and consent management, as applicable. Google's handling and retention of data are described in the Google Privacy Policy. If an in-app “Privacy Choices” option is available, you can use it to review applicable ad-consent choices.

Users with a verified permanent ad-removal entitlement do not receive ad requests from VoxaPair. Data previously processed by Google or Apple remains subject to those providers' policies and your choices with them.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

VoxaPair does not maintain an account, recording archive, or translation history on its own servers. You can stop recording at any time and revoke microphone permission in iOS Settings. You can remove app-stored settings by deleting the app. Purchase records and ad-related data are managed by Apple and Google under their respective policies. For questions or requests about data handled by VoxaPair, contact us at haven.he@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

简体中文

本政策说明 VoxaPair iOS App 如何处理信息。VoxaPair 提供语音识别、文字翻译、语音播报、免费版广告，以及可选的一次性永久去广告购买。

语音与翻译

在获得麦克风权限后，VoxaPair 仅在您使用录音功能时采集语音。App 使用 Apple 系统框架完成语音识别、翻译和播报，并在 App 中显示识别原文和译文。VoxaPair 没有接收或保存录音、原文、译文的自有服务器；VoxaPair 也不会把这些内容提供给 Google 用于广告。App 可能请求 iOS 从 Apple 下载语言资源。

本地设置与内购

已选择的语言组合及资源准备状态保存在设备上。VoxaPair 不要求注册账号。可选的永久去广告内购由 Apple StoreKit 处理，Apple 负责付款信息；VoxaPair 核验购买权益以决定是否展示广告。VoxaPair 不运营独立支付服务器，也不会取得您的银行卡信息。

广告及第三方数据处理

免费版使用 Google AdMob 展示横幅广告，并使用 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）管理适用的广告同意选择。根据您的所在地、同意选择和设备设置，这些服务可能收集或处理由 IP 地址推断的大致位置、设备或 App 标识符、所见广告、App 互动、崩溃信息、性能信息及其他诊断信息。适用时，这些数据可能用于第三方广告、开发者广告或营销、分析及同意管理。Google 的数据处理和保留方式见 Google 隐私权政策。如果 App 中显示“隐私选项”，您可通过该入口查看适用的广告同意选择。

经验证已永久去广告的用户，VoxaPair 不再请求广告。Google 或 Apple 此前处理的数据仍受其各自政策及您对其作出的选择约束。

保留、删除与您的选择

VoxaPair 不在自有服务器上维护账号、录音档案或翻译历史。您可随时停止录音，并可在 iOS“设置”中撤销麦克风权限。删除 App 可移除其存放在设备上的设置。购买记录及广告相关数据分别由 Apple 和 Google 按其政策管理。如需咨询或请求处理 VoxaPair 管理的数据，请发送邮件至 haven.he@gmail.com。

政策更新

当 App 或数据处理方式变化时，我们可能更新本政策；发布修订版时将更新页面顶部的生效日期。
